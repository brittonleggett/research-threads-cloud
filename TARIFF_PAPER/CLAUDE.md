## Sensitivity note
This project involves human-subjects research (IRB application, informed
consent forms). Treat any participant data or identifiable material with
the same care as the IRB protocol requires. When in doubt, ask before
sharing, summarizing externally, or committing anything that isn't
already de-identified/template material.
