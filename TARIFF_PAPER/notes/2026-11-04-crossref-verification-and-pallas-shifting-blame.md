## New lead (not in our literature files)
- Pallas, Bolton & Lobschat (2018), "Shifting the Blame," Journal of Service Research 21(3), 302-318, DOI 10.1177/1094670518755314. Abstract (Crossref): surcharge information drives blame attributions for a price increase; internal-locus surcharges raise blame, external-locus surcharges can make surcharge pricing beneficial versus all-inclusive pricing. This is the closest peer-reviewed precedent for Study 2's chain (who the firm names as responsible -> blame -> response). Relevant to H3 direction thinking: an external cue (government tariff) may help the firm, which cuts against the Morwitz & Fitzsimons reported result that blame shifts but reactions stay negative. Abstract only; full text not read.
