## Greenleaf, Johnson, Morwitz & Shalev review
- Search results consistently show: Journal of Consumer Psychology (not JCR), review of partitioned pricing, DOI 10.1016/j.jcps.2015.04.006. Preprint copies are hosted on Columbia and Wharton sites. Authors match. Print year 2016 (vol. 26(1)) matches the 10-26 note, but volume/pages were not seen in a primary source tonight. Status: authors, journal, DOI supported by search results; volume/pages still unverified.
- Core point to use: partitioned pricing = base price plus mandatory surcharges; consumers tend to underestimate total cost when partitioned. An itemized tariff fits the definition. The review's six-stage framework starts with attention to price components.
