## The finding

The Supreme Court struck down the administration's IEEPA-based tariff authority in February
2026. Tariffs have since been reimposed under different legal bases, in sequence: a Section 122
tariff (which itself expired July 24, 2026), followed by search results pointing to a
Section 301-based layer replacing it. This is a real, multi-step legal shift that happened
*during* this project's timeline.

## Why it matters for TARIFF_PAPER
