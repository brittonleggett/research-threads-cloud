## Design decision: 3×2 between-subjects factorial (locked)

**Attribution Frame** (3 levels: tariff-explicit / general-cost-explicit / silent-no-explanation)
**× Cost-Response** (2 levels: full pass-through / shared-burden absorption)
= 6 cells, single vignette exposure per participant, retail-brand context (anonymized,
per CFP's retail encouragement — echoing Nike/Mattel/Williams-Sonoma framing from the real
Study 1 corpus rather than an unbranded/generic firm).

**Why this design, not the two other candidates that were floating in the files:**
- The lit review's 3-cell design (silence/gain-frame/loss-frame) and the GSRBC submission's
  4-cell design (silence/neutral/gain-framed mitigation/loss-framed consequence) both predate
  Study 1's actual corpus work and don't map onto anything found in it.
- The 3×2 factorial is the one **independently validated by Study 1's coded themes**:
  Attribution Frame = Theme 2 (causation attribution: explicit/vague/silent, present across
  all 7 real corpus artifacts). Cost-Response = Theme 1 + Theme 3 combined (restraint
  signaling + mitigation-effort narrative — i.e., "we're absorbing some of this" vs. full
  pass-through).

**Checked against actual recent JCM methodology (2026-08-04 search, not assumption):**
- Gallen, Brunel & Masson (2025), *JCM* — single 3-level manipulated factor (message type)
  crossed with a measured moderator (diet), N=720, single-scenario exposure, between-subjects.
- Choi, Chan & Fock (2025), *JCM* — multi-study program (3 studies + 1 supplementary), mixed
  field experiments and surveys, cross-sectional + longitudinal — confirms JCM welcomes a
  multi-study program like Britton's existing Study 1/2/3 structure without needing to
  compress it into one study.
- Adjacent price-fairness literature (2024 hotel-pricing study) — 2×2 between-subjects
  scenario design, DV chain of fairness → brand trust/affect → purchase intention/satisfaction
  — near-exact match to the leaner model's mediation chain below.

**Conclusion: the existing 3×2 diagrams were already right-sized for this venue.** No
structural change needed — this note exists to document *why*, with real precedent, not to
propose a new design.
