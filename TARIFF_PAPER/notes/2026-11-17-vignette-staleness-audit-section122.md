## Finding (from the file, not search)
The six "Meridian Home" vignettes (`notes/2026-08-04-vignette-drafts-v1.md`, Cells 1-2) say only "new tariffs on imported goods" and "tariff-related costs." **No statute, authority, country, rate or date is named.** The silent and general-cost cells name nothing about tariffs. So the legal-status churn (IEEPA struck down, Section 122 ruled unlawful by CIT 5/7, stayed by CAFC) does not make the stimulus text factually wrong.
