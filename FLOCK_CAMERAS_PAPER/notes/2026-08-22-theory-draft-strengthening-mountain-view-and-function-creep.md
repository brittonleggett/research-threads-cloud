## Insert 1 — strengthening the Theme 2 (secrecy/contextual-integrity) evidentiary paragraph

**Where:** `Introduction_and_Theory_DRAFT_2026-08-16.md`, the contextual-integrity paragraph under
"Procedural Justice, Contextual Integrity, and the Secrecy Mechanism" (current lines 98-109),
which currently illustrates undisclosed federal data flows with Evanston/Bend/Syracuse and the San
Jose warrantless-search figure. This is the locked primary manipulation axis (Theme 2), so
strengthening its evidentiary base is directly on-strategy and not gated — Theme 2's status as the
primary axis isn't in question, only its supporting detail is being added to.

**Why Mountain View specifically:** the 08-21 addendum's #25 (Mountain View, CA) is, by the
addendum's own assessment, "the single most granular 'who exactly accessed the data, and how many
cameras' account in the corpus so far" — and it adds a mechanism variant the original six Theme-2
artifacts didn't have: a **state-level**, not just federal-level, version of the same undisclosed-
default problem (California law-enforcement agencies accessing 29 of the city's 30 cameras via a
separate statewide search function, alongside the federal agencies). That's a genuine addition to
the theoretical point being made (undisclosed default access is a structural pattern in the
technology, not a one-off federal-only quirk), not just another example of the same mechanism.

**Proposed additional sentences** (to insert after the existing "...it is that data moved outside
the context the public understood it to be confined to." sentence, before "We treat undisclosed
default data-sharing as a contextual-integrity violation..."):

> "The mechanism is not limited to federal access. In Mountain View, California, an internal
> audit found that a single camera's data had been queried by federal agencies as geographically
> and functionally disparate as an Air Force base, a national recreation area, and multiple
> regional field offices — while a separate, distinct search function had let California
> state and local law-enforcement agencies access data from twenty-nine of the city's thirty
> cameras, neither use approved by the city or its police department. That a single undisclosed-
> default architecture produces both a federal and a state-level version of the same violation is,
> if anything, stronger evidence that the mechanism under study is structural to how the
> technology is configured by default, not an artifact of any one agency's federal overreach."

**Citation-confidence note, to carry into the manuscript if this is adopted:** per the 08-21
addendum, Mountain View is WebSearch-triangulated across three outlets (ABC7 News, Yahoo, AOL),
not yet direct-fetch verified the way corpus items #1-16 were. Recommend a direct-fetch check of
the ABC7 News piece specifically (the original reporting outlet, not an aggregator) before this
goes into a submitted manuscript, consistent with how #20/#21 were handled in the same addendum.

## Insert 2 — updating the Theme 3 "not built into primary model" note for accuracy, without
promoting it

**Where:** `Introduction_and_Theory_DRAFT_2026-08-16.md`, "A Note on Themes Not Built Into the
Primary Model" (current lines 213-226), specifically the function-creep sentence: *"a
function-creep pattern... is real but rests on only two, thinly sourced corpus artifacts... and is
treated as a discussion-section direction for future work rather than a hypothesis in this
design."*

**Why this needs updating regardless of what Britton decides on promotion:** the sentence makes a
factual claim ("rests on only two, thinly sourced corpus artifacts") that is now simply false — it
rests on five, three of which are named, adjudicated-or-charged, multi-outlet-sourced cases
(Menasha's Cristian Morales, sentenced to jail time; Milwaukee's Josue Ayala and Tehrangi Chapman,
both charged). Leaving the sentence as-is means the manuscript would understate its own evidentiary
base even if Britton decides *not* to promote Theme 3 into the primary design — the "kept as a
discussion-section direction" decision can still stand on updated, accurate grounds. This is a
factual-accuracy correction to a description of existing evidence, not a design decision, so it
doesn't require his sign-off the way promoting Theme 3 into a hypothesis would.

**Proposed replacement sentence** (same "reported for completeness, not a hypothesis" framing
preserved; promotion question explicitly left open rather than pre-decided):

> "First, a function-creep pattern — the network sold as a stolen-vehicle tool but used for
> reproductive-healthcare surveillance, personal stalking, or unauthorized off-duty tracking — is
> real and, as of a 2026-08-21 corpus addendum, now supported by five artifacts rather than the
> two thinly sourced cases in the original 22-artifact corpus: alongside the original two, a
> Menasha, Wisconsin officer was sentenced to six months in jail and permanently barred from law
> enforcement or Flock system access for using the network to track an ex-girlfriend, and two
> Milwaukee officers were separately charged with misconduct for tracking people they were
> personally involved with, one case surfaced only after the victim checked a public self-audit
> tool (haveibeenflocked.com). A cited but not yet independently verified industry-tracking figure
> (attributed by search results to the Institute for Justice: at least 21 similar cases nationally
> since 2024) would, if confirmed, convert this from an anecdotal pattern into a documented
> national one. This strengthened evidentiary base is treated here as a discussion-section
> direction for future work rather than a hypothesis in the present design, consistent with the
> 2026-08-16 Phase 3 decision — though the decision to keep it out of the primary Study 2 model
> was made when the evidentiary base was thinner, and is flagged for the PI's own reassessment
> given how much stronger it now is (see `notes/2026-08-21-corpus-addendum-new-evidence.md` and
> `notes/2026-08-22-theory-draft-strengthening-mountain-view-and-function-creep.md`)."

This preserves the exact "not promoted" outcome of the 08-16 lock while (a) fixing the now-false
"only two" claim, (b) carrying the new evidence's own citation-confidence caveats forward (the
21-case IJ figure is explicitly marked unverified, not stated as fact), and (c) pointing whoever
reads the manuscript draft next — Britton or a future session — straight at the still-open
promotion question rather than either silently deciding it or silently leaving stale text in
place.
