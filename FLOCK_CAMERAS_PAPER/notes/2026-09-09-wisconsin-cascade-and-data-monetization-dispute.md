**Wisconsin regional cascade (#40):** Searching for the vote context surfaced a much bigger story —
Fond du Lac County and Winnebago County (nine police departments plus both sheriff's offices

records requests, to publish undercover officers' names and active-investigation details alongside
personal contact information. This is a genuinely new mechanism for the corpus — not ICE/Border-
Patrol access (#36) and not the sunshine-law-forces-disclosure pattern (#32), but public-records

officer names or plate numbers) — direct-fetched from FOX6 Milwaukee, one of very few places in
this whole corpus where Flock itself concedes a specific problem rather than issuing a blanket
denial or generic safety-benefit statement.

**Kenosha (#41):** A genuinely different kind of case — not a return to no-ALPR, but a same-

**Racine County (#42):** Sheriff Christopher Schmaling ordered immediate camera removal after
