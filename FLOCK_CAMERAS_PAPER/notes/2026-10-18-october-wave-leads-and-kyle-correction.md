# 2026-10-18 — October removal wave: 4 new leads, Kyle TX needs a correction

Method: one extended web search for current Flock council votes, then direct fetches of the articles. Four fetched (Midland, South Windsor, Cumberland, Milford); the Kyle and Columbia pages returned 403, so Kyle rests on search results only. No Phase 3 or theme changes; this is lead capture. Rows are in `04_DATA/candidate_leads.csv` (F029-F033) and `watchlist.csv`.

- **Midland TX (F029), Oct 13:** 7-0 to terminate; 71 cameras; at-will termination with 60 days' notice; no early-termination fee. The only stated reason in the article is cost (~$225,500 for a second year). No privacy language and no public comment reported. A cost-framed exit rather than a resident-opposition one, so it is a useful contrast for the Texas trigger coding (public comment vs. money).
- **South Windsor CT (F030), ~Oct 6:** 6-3. No hearing speaker favored keeping the cameras. Dissenters argued police-tool value; chief claimed 100+ arrests. Removal in 60 days, data purged, and any return needs a new hearing and vote.
- **Cumberland RI (F031), ~Oct 7:** 6-1 on Resolution 26-45A. Contract had already lapsed. All five hearing speakers opposed, including an ACLU-RI board member. Possibly a new state for the corpus.
- **Milford MI (F032), Oct 5:** 7-0 non-renewal. 600+ petition signatures and weekly protests. The police chief's own reason was vendor responsiveness (outages, vandalism, slow answers), not privacy, and he still called the cameras useful.
- **Kyle TX (F033), correction proposed:** F008 says the council "rejects terminating 6-1." Current search results (KUT, Community Impact) say the 6-1 vote was an earlier one to continue, and on Oct 5 the council voted 4-3 to suspend the cameras until a non-binding May 2027 referendum, with the mayor reportedly switching. Texas Scorecard and KXAN headlines say "cover." This changes Kyle from a clean negative case to a split-then-flip case. Not edited into the corpus; the sources disagree on wording and I could not fetch any of them.
