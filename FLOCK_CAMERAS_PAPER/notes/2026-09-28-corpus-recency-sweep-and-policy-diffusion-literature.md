## 2. Policy-diffusion/termination literature — two real, well-fitting citations found and verified

- **Shipan, C. R., & Volden, C. (2008). "The Mechanisms of Policy Diffusion." *American Journal of
  Political Science*, 52(4), 840–857.** DOI `10.1111/j.1540-5907.2008.00346.x`, confirmed real via
  Crossref (1,053 citing works per Crossref's own count). Identifies four diffusion mechanisms
  (learning, economic competition, imitation, coercion) via 675 U.S. cities' antismoking-policy
  adoption, 1975–2000 — a clean, citable typology for the corpus's own cascade structure.
- **Krause, R. M., Yi, H., & Feiock, R. C. (2016). "Applying Policy Termination Theory to the
  Abandonment of Climate Protection Initiatives by U.S. Local Governments." *Policy Studies
  Journal*, 44(2), 176–195.** DOI `10.1111/psj.12117`, confirmed real via Crossref. A closer
  structural fit than Shipan & Volden: it's about local governments **terminating a program they
  themselves had previously adopted** (climate initiatives), the same basic shape as this corpus's
  rejection wave (adopt Flock, then reject it) — most policy-diffusion literature is about new
  adoption, not termination/abandonment, so this is a genuinely better-matched citation, not just
  an adjacent one. Published in *Policy Studies Journal*, itself one of this paper's own candidate
  target venues.
