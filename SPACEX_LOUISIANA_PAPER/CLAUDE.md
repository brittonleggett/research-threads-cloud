## Real-world grounding (facts gathered 2026-08-27 via WebSearch, see notes/
for sources — not yet WebFetch-primary-verified)
- Announced 2026-08-25: SpaceX + Gov. Landry, second Starship spaceport,
  Vermilion Parish, ~130,000 acres of former Exxon land near Pecan Island.
  $100B investment, construction starting 2027, first launch targeted 2029,
  billed as "the largest spaceport in the world."
- Jobs framing gap: Musk's own video says "probably 10,000 really exciting
  jobs"; Louisiana Economic Development's official estimate is more specific
  and lower — 3,000+ direct jobs (avg. $92,600/yr) plus ~8,100 indirect.
- Fiscal terms: $25M/year flat payment to local taxing authorities in lieu of
  property tax, $20M upfront payment, $100M pledged to the state's coastal
  master plan. LED separately claims 800M+ in direct local payments framing.
- Environmental fight, live now: Louisiana Wildlife Federation, National
  Wildlife Federation, and Pontchartrain Conservancy have filed comments
  opposing an FAA rule change that would waive environmental-review
  requirements for the site — citing whooping crane, piping plover, and
  Kemp's ridley sea turtle habitat in the coastal wetlands. SpaceX has
  separately stated it will keep large portions of the acreage undeveloped
  and is coordinating with Louisiana's CPRA on habitat/hydrology work — a
  voluntary, unverified-by-regulation commitment running alongside the push
  to remove the regulatory mechanism that would otherwise check it.
- **Comparison case already exists**: SpaceX's Starbase, Boca Chica, TX
  (operating since 2021) ran the same playbook — Cameron County's own $800M
  economic-impact claim vs. reporting/ethnographic research (Latino and
  Indigenous community members) on road closures, lost beach access, sonic-
  boom disruption, and local officials' personal real-estate/campaign gains
  outpacing broader community economic gains. That existing work reads as
  sociology/environmental-justice/investigative journalism — no marketing or
  PLS-SEM treatment of the framing-claim-vs-outcome gap specifically was
  found (checked 2026-08-27). That is the open lane.
