# 2026-08-27 — Orientation: SpaceX Vermilion Parish Spaceport Paper

Exploratory. Nothing below is locked — this is the same stage the Data Center

## The real-world hook (why this is timely, not a stretch)

**Announcement (Aug 25, 2026):** SpaceX + Gov. Jeff Landry announced a second
Starship spaceport on ~130,000 acres of former Exxon property in coastal
Vermilion Parish, near the unincorporated community of Pecan Island — a $100B
investment, construction starting 2027, first Starship launch targeted 2029,
billed by SpaceX as "the largest spaceport in the world," with capacity for
thousands of launches annually.
([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/25/spacex-will-build-a-second-100b-starbase-spaceport-in-louisiana/);
[CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/25/spacex-louisiana-spaceport.html);
[CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/08/25/science/spacex-launch-site-louisiana))

**Framing gap #1 — job-number specificity.** Musk's own announcement video:
"probably 10,000 really exciting jobs." Louisiana Economic Development's
official estimate: 3,000+ direct jobs (avg. $92,600/yr) plus ~8,100 indirect
— more specific, sourced, and roughly a third of Musk's round number if read
as "direct" jobs, though the two aren't strictly comparable framings (direct
vs. total, or Musk simply rounding up total to a nicer story).
([Opportunity Louisiana / LED](https://www.opportunitylouisiana.gov/spacex))

**Fiscal terms:** SpaceX pays local taxing authorities $25M/year flat instead
of property tax, plus a $20M upfront payment and $100M pledged to Louisiana's
coastal master plan. LED's own materials separately frame the deal as
800M+ in direct local payments.

**Framing gap #2 — environmental commitment vs. regulatory-waiver push,
happening in parallel, right now.** The Louisiana Wildlife Federation,
National Wildlife Federation, and Pontchartrain Conservancy have filed
comments opposing a proposed FAA rule change that would waive environmental-
review requirements for the site — citing threatened/endangered species
dependent on the coastal wetlands (whooping crane, piping plover, Kemp's
ridley sea turtle).
([Louisiana Illuminator](https://lailluminator.com/2026/08/25/wildlife-spacex/);
[Louisiana Radio Network](https://louisianaradionetwork.com/2026/08/27/47951/);
[Gizmodo](https://gizmodo.com/spacexs-plan-to-build-the-worlds-biggest-spaceport-could-be-an-environmental-disaster-2000803286))
SpaceX has separately stated it will keep large portions of the ~130,000
acres undeveloped and is coordinating with Louisiana's CPRA on habitat and
hydrology work — a voluntary, PR-facing commitment running alongside an
active push to remove the regulatory mechanism that would otherwise verify
it. This is a live instance of the same shape as a greenwashing critique:
public environmental-care language paired with lobbying against the check on
that claim.

**The built-in comparison case — rare for a scouted idea.** SpaceX has run
this exact playbook before, at Starbase, Boca Chica, TX, operating since
2021. Cameron County's own economic-impact claim there is $800M+; separately,
investigative/ethnographic work with Latino and Indigenous multigenerational
community members has documented road closures blocking beach access, sonic-
boom disruption to human and wildlife populations, and reporting that local
politicians' personal real-estate holdings and campaign budgets gained more
than the region's broader economy did.
([The Conversation](https://theconversation.com/the-starbase-rocket-testing-facility-is-permanently-changing-the-landscape-of-southern-texas-242450);
search summary of ongoing sociology/environmental-justice field research
since 2021 — not yet independently pulled in full text, flag before citing
directly.) Checked 2026-08-27: no marketing-journal or PLS-SEM treatment of
the framing-claim-vs-outcome gap at Boca Chica was found — the existing
academic/investigative attention there is sociology, environmental justice,
and journalism, not a marketing-discipline treatment. That is the open lane
this paper would fill, and Louisiana gives a live, real-time second site to
pair it with rather than a purely retrospective single-case study.

## Candidate theoretical frames (pick one primary chain, not all of these)

The recurring shape in Britton's other work is antecedent → psychological
mediator → downstream outcome, with a moderator that reshapes the
relationship (see `user_research_corpus.md`). Candidates:

- **Claim-specificity / attribution framing** (direct extension of TARIFF_
  PAPER's Theme 2) — vague, round, informal benefit claims (Musk's "10,000
  exciting jobs") vs. precise, sourced ones (LED's official breakdown).
  Mediator candidate: perceived credibility/trustworthiness of the claim.
- **Greenwashing / CSR-claim verifiability** — voluntary environmental
  commitment language paired with active opposition to the regulatory review
  that would verify it. Mediator candidates: perceived sincerity, perceived
  manipulative intent (a well-established construct in the greenwashing
  literature).
- **Procedural justice / regulatory-venue-shifting** (direct reuse of
  DATA_CENTER_PAPER's established code) — same mechanism, federal/FAA level
  instead of state utility-commission level.
- **Precedent/history-informed skepticism** — does prior knowledge of how
  Boca Chica's promises actually played out change how the identical playbook
  lands the second time, in a different state? This is the paper's most
  distinctive possible contribution — a "does the same script work twice"
  question that a single-site study structurally cannot ask.

**Strongest candidate primary model** (mirrors the Data Center Paper's
"strongest candidate" framing so it's directly comparable in structure):
benefit-claim specificity and environmental-commitment verifiability (both
manipulable/codeable) **→** perceived sincerity / greenwashing perception
**→** trust in company and/or state government **→** public support /
opposition intention, **moderated by** prior awareness of the Boca Chica
outcome (does knowing the Texas precedent change how the Louisiana version of
the same claims is received).

This is a proposal, not a lock.

## Candidate Study 1 designs (the actual open decision)

Same three-way split established for the Data Center Paper:

**Option A — corporate/official messaging analysis (Tariff-paper-style).**
Corpus: SpaceX/Musk statements, LED and Gov. Landry materials, FAA docket
filings from SpaceX's side. Builds a typology of how the company and state
frame benefit claims and environmental commitments — parallels Tariff's
Theme 2 almost exactly, applied to benefit-framing instead of cost-
attribution.

**Option B — opposition/public discourse analysis (CCS-paper-style,
netnography).** Corpus: wildlife-group filings (Louisiana Wildlife
Federation, National Wildlife Federation, Pontchartrain Conservancy), local
news coverage quoting Vermilion Parish/Pecan Island residents, and the
existing Boca Chica sociology/journalism literature as a comparison corpus.
Builds a typology of objection frames actually used by opponents and
affected communities.

**Option C — both, paired, with Boca Chica as an explicit second site**
(mirrors the Data Center Paper's Option C reasoning almost exactly): lets
Study 1 report a framing-mismatch finding on its own (what the company/state
claim vs. what opposition groups and comparable-precedent outcomes show) —
publishable groundwork before any Study 2 vignette-survey work, and the
comparative Louisiana/Boca Chica structure is the paper's strongest
differentiator from a single-site study.
