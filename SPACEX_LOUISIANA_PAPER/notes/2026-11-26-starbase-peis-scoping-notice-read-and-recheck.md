# 2026-11-26 — Starbase PEIS scoping notice (read in full) and watchlist recheck

## Verified (primary source, read)
Federal Register public-inspection PDF 2026-20963 (FAA, docket **FAA-2026-12808**, scheduled publication 2026-10-14), downloaded and text-extracted:
- Notice of Intent to prepare a **Programmatic EIS** for SpaceX Starship-Super Heavy operations at **Boca Chica / Starbase, Texas** plus expansion of landing and expenditure locations.
- Currently authorized: 25 launches / 50 landings a year. PEIS evaluates up to **456 launches and 912 landings a year** (Phase 1: 100 launches / 200 landings; Phase 2: the full 456/912).
- Scoping comments due **2026-11-30**. Four in-person meetings (Brownsville and Port Isabel, Nov 17 and 19) and one virtual (Nov 18, 5:30-7:30 pm Central). An earlier search summary said all three were virtual; the notice itself says otherwise, so use the notice.
- The notice text I extracted does not mention Louisiana.
