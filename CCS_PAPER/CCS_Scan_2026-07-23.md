## Recent Real-World Developments

- **Louisiana now holds EPA Class VI primacy** (granted per Carbon Capture Coalition statement) — Louisiana is the third state (after North Dakota, Wyoming) with primacy to permit CO2 injection wells itself, and Texas followed with its own primacy approval in Nov 2025. This is a significant regulatory shift since Track A's corpus was assembled — worth checking whether the corpus needs a 2026 supplement. [Carbon Capture Coalition statement](https://carboncapturecoalition.org/blog/carbon-capture-coalition-statement-on-epa-granting-louisiana-primacy-over-class-vi-wells-for-geologic-storage-of-carbon-dioxide/) · [Oil & Gas Journal](https://www.ogj.com/general-interest/government/article/14303412/epa-decision-to-give-louisiana-permit-rights-could-lead-to-carbon-capture-surge)
- **New permits actively being issued in 2026**: Hackberry Carbon Sequestration got the first LA-issued Class VI permit (Sept 2025); Strategic Biofuels got a draft Class VI permit for a multi-well project, the first of its kind in the state. [Carbon Herald](https://carbonherald.com/louisiana-grants-hackberry-carbon-sequestration-class-vi-well-permit/) · [Strategic Biofuels press release](https://strategicbiofuels.com/news/strategic-biofuels-secures-draft-class-vi-well-permit/)
- **Public dispute over CCS claims (Feb 2026)**: Louisiana's Dept. of Conservation and Energy publicly disputed an Environmental Integrity Project report critical of the state's CCS oversight, stating its Class VI program is "the most stringent... in the nation." This back-and-forth is itself potential Track A corpus material (agency rebuttal document). [KALB coverage](https://www.kalb.com/2026/02/23/ce-disputes-eip-report-about-carbon-capture-louisiana/) · [LA Dept. of Conservation and Energy rebuttal](https://www.dce.louisiana.gov/news/facts-refute-misleading-claims-ccs-projects)
- **Tribal consultation tension is live, not historical.** ExxonMobil has proposed CO2 injection wells less than a mile from the Coushatta reservation; young Coushatta members publicly spoke out in 2025 after state lawmakers voted down a bill important to their land/culture/safety, and environmental groups have called industry claims of adequate community consultation "disingenuous and dishonest." **More bills on this were prefiled for the 2026 legislative session** — this is directly relevant to Track B's recognition-justice/Tribal-sovereignty framing and is an active, ongoing story, not settled history. [Louisiana Illuminator, Coushatta](https://lailluminator.com/2025/04/30/coushatta-carbon/) · [Verite News, Feb 2026](https://veritenews.org/2026/02/12/louisiana-carbon-capture-projects/) · [Louisiana Illuminator, Feb 2026](https://lailluminator.com/2026/02/11/louisianas-nation-leading-proposed-carbon-capture-projects-alarm-environmentalists/)
