# 2026-11-02 Summit Iowa permit amendment: already decided in July

The watchlist item (created 2026-10-09) treated the IUC decision as pending. Search shows it was decided about 2026-07-02 (DTN; Carbon Herald; Iowa Farm Bureau dated 7/13). The IUC removed the condition tying Iowa construction to ND/SD approvals, replaced it with a requirement to show approvals for a route to a sequestration site, kept the construction ban until then, and denied motions to dismiss. Summit's 2024 permit order is still under judicial review in Polk County.

Source level: search summaries only; the IUC order was not read, and the date rests mainly on one source. Logged as L006 in 04_DATA/candidate_leads.csv.

Why it matters for the paper: it is a regulator loosening a local-state-approval condition while keeping a construction ban, which is a useful "implementing body" data point for the CCS design.
