# 2026-08-28 — $1M fallback finally primary-source verified; Xiao et al. year resolved via CrossRef; HB804 still quiet

## 1. HB79's $250K/$500K/$1M noneconomic-damages caps: now PRIMARY-VERIFIED, confirms the 08-27 finding exactly

Direct-fetched the actual bill PDF from `legis.la.gov` (HLS 26RS-572, HB79, 2026 Regular
Session, by Rep. Robby Carter) — this succeeded where WebFetch had been blocked for 17+ prior
sessions. Full text confirms, unambiguously, in the bill's own operative language and House
Legislative Services digest:

- **Present law (pre-HB79)**, i.e. R.S. 30:1109(B) as it stood before this bill, already set:
  - $250,000/person general cap on noneconomic-loss damages
  - $500,000/person cap for exceptional cases (wrongful death, permanent substantial physical
    deformity, loss of limb/organ, permanent injury preventing independent self-care)
  - $1,000,000/person fallback if either of the above caps is ever found unconstitutional
- **HB79 amends R.S. 30:1109(B)-(F) and repeals R.S. 30:1109(G)**, striking all three dollar
  figures from the statute. The digest states plainly: "Proposed law removes the present law
  limitation of liability on any damages resulting from carbon capture and storage."

This is a full repeal of pre-existing per-person caps, not a new cap being created — matching
the 08-21/08-27 WebSearch-based conclusion exactly, now confirmed against primary statutory
text rather than search summaries. One caveat worth flagging: the fetched PDF is stamped
"ORIGINAL" (HLS 26RS-572), i.e. the bill as introduced, not necessarily the final enrolled Act
614 text — if any floor amendments changed this language before passage, that wouldn't show
here. Worth one more check against the enrolled/Act version before citing this at
manuscript-final precision, though the House digest's plain-English abstract (which describes
intent, not just this draft's text) makes a late substantive reversal unlikely.

**Source (primary, directly fetched):** [HB79 bill text, legis.la.gov](https://www.legis.la.gov/legis/ViewDocument.aspx?d=1436661)
