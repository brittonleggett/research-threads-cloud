# 2026-08-04 — Track A corpus supplement candidates

Overnight pass addressing the staleness flag from the 2026-07-23 scan. **One correction to
that scan first:** it framed "Louisiana now holds EPA Class VI primacy" as a new development
since the corpus was assembled — that's not quite right. The actual primacy grant happened
via Federal Register decision in **January 2024** (EPA press release December 2023), and
`Corpus_1/` already contains both `Federal_Register.pdf` and the specifically-named
`Federal Register __ State of Louisiana Underground Injection Control Program; Class VI
Primacy.pdf` — so that document is very likely already in the corpus, not missing from it.
What's actually new since 2024 is what Louisiana has *done* with that authority, and the
political fight over it — that's the real supplement material below.

## Genuinely new-since-assembly documents found

**1. DCE public rebuttal (Feb 2026)** — "Facts Refute Misleading Claims About Louisiana's
Carbon Sequestration Projects," Louisiana Dept. of Conservation and Energy.
[dce.louisiana.gov](https://www.dce.louisiana.gov/news/facts-refute-misleading-claims-ccs-projects)
— agency rebuttal, directly quotable, not in the existing corpus.

**2. EIP report (Feb 11, 2026)** — Environmental Integrity Project report that DCE's rebuttal
above responds to: ≥65 proposed CCS projects in Louisiana (most of any state), 11 CO2
pipelines, ~33M metric tons CO2/year combined. This is the "other side" of a live public
dispute — corpus balance matters here, not just the agency's own framing.
[Louisiana Illuminator coverage](https://lailluminator.com/2026/02/11/louisianas-nation-leading-proposed-carbon-capture-projects-alarm-environmentalists/)

**3. New 2026-session bills, beyond the HB5 already in the corpus:**
- **HB6** — Rapides Parish-specific version of HB5's local-control mechanism.
- **HB7** — Louisiana Landowners Protection Act; repeals the CCS unitization statute,
  removes expropriation authority for CCS projects/pipelines. Substantively different from
  HB5/HB6 — worth its own coding pass, not a minor variant.
- **HB820** (HLS 26RS-512) — found via direct legislature link, not yet reviewed for content.
- **Context: 40+ total CCS-related bills filed this session** — HB5/6/7/820 are a sample, not
  the full picture. A future session should do a systematic pull of all 40+ from the
  legislature site rather than relying on news coverage to surface individual bills, if Track
  A gets picked up in earnest.
[Liskow & Lewis legislative update](https://www.liskow.com/insights/three-ccs-bills-prefiled-for-2026-louisiana-regular-session-102mh1k/)

**4. Two new Class VI permits, both post-2024-primacy actions:**
- **Hackberry Carbon Sequestration** (Cameron Parish) — first draft permit issued since
  LDENR received primacy.
- **Strategic Biofuels / Louisiana Green Fuels** (Caldwell Parish) — first multi-well draft
  permit, part of a combined power-plant-plus-storage project; public hearing was scheduled
  for 2026-04-09, so a final-approval document may now exist too — worth checking current
  status when this is picked up.
[Carbon Herald — Hackberry](https://carbonherald.com/louisiana-grants-hackberry-carbon-sequestration-class-vi-well-permit/) · [Strategic Biofuels press release](https://strategicbiofuels.com/news/strategic-biofuels-secures-draft-class-vi-well-permit/)
