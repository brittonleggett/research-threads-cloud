# 2026-11-22 — Brazil Section 301 beef exemption (primary read) and Tyson DPP recheck

## Primary-source finding
Federal Register vol. 91 no. 137 (2026-07-20), USTR Section 301 Brazil action, https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2026-07-20/html/2026-14542.htm (fetched; first 100,000 of ~112,000 characters read).
- 25% additional duty (heading 9903.05.01) effective 12:01 am ET 2026-07-22.
- U.S. note 50(a)(ii) exempts chapter 02 subheadings including 0201.10.05 through 0202.30.80 (fresh/frozen beef) via heading 9903.05.03. So **Brazilian beef is NOT hit by the 301 duty.**
- USTR declined comments asking it to drop beef from the exemption (kept everything except high-purity dissolving pulp).
- The text read says nothing on 26.4%. Reading was via WebFetch summarization; the exact note wording should be checked by a human.

## Implication for the 26.4% watchlist item
Secondary trade press (S&P Global, Beef Central, Expana) says Brazil's out-of-quota beef rate is 26.4% after its "other countries" TRQ (52,005 MT for 2026) fills, and that the separate 40% surcharge was dropped Nov 2025. Not primary-confirmed in HTSUS chapter 99/chapter 2. Do not cite 26.4% without HTSUS.
