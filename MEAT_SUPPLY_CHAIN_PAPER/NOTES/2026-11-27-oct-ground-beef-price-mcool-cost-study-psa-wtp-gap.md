## 1. October 2026 ground beef (BLS 100% ground beef, FRED APU0000703112)
WebFetch of the FRED text file (a model summary of the data, not me reading raw rows) gives: Aug 6.923, Sep 6.792, **Oct 6.755** ($/lb). So Aug->Oct is -2.4%, with Sep->Oct only -0.5%. Mar-Oct range is 6.70-6.92. This continues the pattern already logged: retail ground beef did not rise after Proclamation 11059's 300k mt quota expansion, but it did not fall much either. One data point per month, no controls (cattle prices, seasonality, other tariffs). This is observational color, not an estimate of pass-through. Re-verify the numbers in the FRED UI before any manuscript use.
