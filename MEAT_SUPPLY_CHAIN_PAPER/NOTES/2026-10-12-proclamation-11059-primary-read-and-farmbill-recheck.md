## Proclamation 11059 (Claim #7 natural experiment) — primary-source read
Read via govinfo (FR Doc. 2026-17842, 91 FR 55989-55994, published 2026-08-31; signed 2026-08-26). Reading was done through
a fetch tool that summarizes pages, so exact wording below is as returned, not a line-by-line proofread.
- Authority: Sec. 404 Uruguay Round Agreements Act; sec. 604 Trade Act of 1974; 3 U.S.C. 301.
- Adds **300,000 mt** to the 2026 in-quota amount for lean beef trimmings (HTS 0201.30.5091, .5097; 0202.30.5091, .5097),
  allocated entirely to "other countries or areas." Argentina's earlier increase (Proclamation 11010) is left untouched.
- Tranches, first come first served: 100,000 mt Sep 1-30; 100,000 mt Oct 1-30; 100,000 mt opens Oct 31, closes when filled
  or Nov 30. **Tranche 2 ends Oct 30, so the window is in progress now.**
- Price condition: USDA and USTR monitor whether the imports sell "at a price 25 percent below the market price for lean
  beef trimmings"; if not, they notify the President, who may end what remains. Paragraph 7: if the action does not lower
  the sale price of imported ground beef, the President "may end the action."

**Correction to carry (M004):** trade press (Meatingplace, Beef Magazine, MercoPress) describes this as a "waiver of the 26.4%
out-of-quota duty." The proclamation text, as read, raises the **in-quota** quantity; imports within it pay the lower in-quota
rate. The practical effect for Brazil is the same direction, but the mechanism is a quota expansion, not a rate change.
Also: MercoPress says the Argentina quota rose to 100,000 mt for 2026 (Feb 6 proclamation); Beef Magazine says 11010 added
80,000 mt. These are compatible only if the base was 20,000 mt; I did not read 11010 itself tonight. Not resolved.

**Early price signal (trade-press level only, S&P Global/Platts snippets):** Brazil 90CL lean trimmings FCA East Coast $3.03/lb on
Sep 1, down 25 cents day-on-day; Australian lean-90s down ~A$1.40/kg over five weeks. Part of the Brazilian drop was for October
delivery. This is import-side trimmings price, not retail. Previously logged retail ground beef move ($7.29 -> $7.13, -2%) is
still the only retail evidence; no September retail data found. Tranche fill rates: not found.
