# Evidence Table

Master claim → source verification ledger. Every load-bearing empirical claim
used elsewhere in this project (NOTES, LITERATURE, ANALYSIS, STUDY* memos,
eventually MANUSCRIPT) should trace back to a row here. Add new rows at the
bottom of the relevant commodity section; do not delete superseded rows —
mark them superseded and explain why in Notes.

Verification status values: `Verified (primary)`, `Verified (secondary,
traced to primary)`, `Could not verify`, `Contradicted by other source`,
`Pending`.

Columns: Claim | Source | Source type | URL | Pub. date | Page/Table |
Commodity | Years covered | Geography | Verification status | Notes
