## What this is

news-sourced coverage of Meta's Hyperion project — zero primary documents (minutes,
filings, permits, contracts). This is the anchor case for the whole paper and, per

## Found: two real LPSC dockets, with a docket number and filing date each

- **Docket U-37882** — Entergy Louisiana's 7-new-gas-plant expansion to power Hyperion.
  Already the subject of the secrecy-vote note (`2026-09-10-tier1-louisiana-primary-
  source-pull-and-xai-ruling-recheck.md`); confirmed today via a second source
  (LouisianaAI.com's docket tracker) that the LPSC's own procedural schedule targets a
  final decision by **December 16, 2026** — a real, checkable future event for the
  corpus, not a settled outcome yet.
- **Docket U-37425** — a **Motion for Investigation of the New Financial Arrangement of
  Meta Platforms LLC** + a **Motion to Institute Prudence Review of Entergy**, filed
  **January 14, 2026** by the Alliance for Affordable Energy and the Union of Concerned
  Scientists, represented by Earthjustice. This is new to the corpus. Core allegation:
  Meta restructured its ownership of the Hyperion project into a Delaware special-purpose
  vehicle, **"Beignet Investors,"** registered **August 20, 2025 — the same day the LPSC
  approved Entergy's original gas-plant buildout** — reducing Meta's own stake to 20%
  (private equity investors hold the other 80%), which the motion argues could let Meta
  "walk away from the data center in four years" while ratepayers remain on the hook for
  gas plants built with a 30-year expected lifespan. Source: Earthjustice's own press
  release (`earthjustice.org/press/2026/louisiana-regulators-asked-to-investigate-shady-
  meta-financing-deal...`), which names the docket, filers, and date directly.
  **Not independently verified against the actual motion text** — the underlying PDF
  (mirrored at `all4energy.org`) downloaded fine but this environment's PDF tooling
  (`pdftoppm`/poppler-utils) is still broken, same gap flagged in multiple notes this
  week, confirmed again today on a fresh attempt. Treat the docket number, filer names,
  and date as primary-source-confirmed (from Earthjustice's own release, itself a primary
  party to the filing); treat the specific dollar/percentage claims as accurately
  characterized by that release but not independently re-read from the filing itself.

## Found: a real regulatory-capture finding — Entergy ghostwrote PSC commissioners' own motions

WWNO (2026-08-11) reports that an Entergy Louisiana VP, **Larry Hand**, authored or
substantially rewrote LPSC motions that commissioners then read into the record as their
own — for **both** the Meta docket (August 20, 2025 vote) and a separate Hyundai Steel
mill docket (Ascension Parish, March 2025). Evidence cited: Word-document metadata naming
Hand as author, and an email from Hand to Commissioner Foster Campbell reading "As
discussed, attached is motion to consider for the Meta docket" — the motion that followed
matched Hand's draft "almost word for word." WWNO links the email itself via DocumentCloud
(embedded in the article, not independently re-fetched here). **This is a genuinely
significant addition** — a documented instance of a Louisiana utility writing the regulatory
body's own approval language for this exact project, directly relevant to any
manufactured-legitimacy or regulatory-capture theme already in the thematic map. Not yet
added as a corpus row — that's a sizing/inclusion call per standing rule, flagging for
Britton/Phase 3 rather than deciding it.

## Found: the LDEQ air/water permits, filed under a different corporate name

LDEQ approved the facility's initial air permit **July 2025** under **"Laidley LLC,"** the
real-estate entity holding a 20% share of the project (not "Meta" directly) — 31
emergency diesel generators, a diesel fire-protection system, diesel storage tanks. Amended
**January 14, 2026** to add 44 natural-gas water boilers (24/7 operation); LDEQ approved the
amendment **June 6, 2026**. Water permit: **~23 million gallons/day across six wells**,
sized to peak summer cooling demand. Important scope note found in the same reporting: this
permit does **not** cover the 10 gas turbines Entergy itself is building — Entergy is
separately responsible for permitting those (i.e., the LPSC dockets above and the LDEQ
permit are genuinely separate regulatory tracks, not duplicative). Source: Louisiana
Illuminator's 2026-08-04 Sierra Club coverage, which itself is reporting on the actual LDEQ
permit record — the underlying LDEQ filing/permit document itself was not directly located
or fetched.
