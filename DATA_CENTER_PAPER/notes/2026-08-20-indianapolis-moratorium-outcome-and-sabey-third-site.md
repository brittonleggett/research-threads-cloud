## 1. The open item: Indianapolis City-County Council data-center moratorium — RESOLVED, it passed and is now final

08-19's note left this as the single most actionable open thread: a moratorium-through-2027 had
advanced out of committee for a full-council vote scheduled Aug 10, outcome unconfirmed. Now
confirmed via WebSearch, and it's gone further than just the council vote:

- **Aug 10, 2026: full City-County Council passed it 23-1.** Council President Maggie Lewis
  (D, westside) framed it as buying time to "fine-tune" city regulation — campus size, building
  height, sound levels, environmental impacts — via a study committee of experts and residents.
  [WFYI](https://www.wfyi.org/wfyi-news/2026-08-10/indy-council-advances-pause-on-data-centers-sends-to-commission-for-final-approval), [IBJ](https://www.ibj.com/articles/city-county-council-votes-to-establish-data-center-moratorium)
- **Aug 19, 2026 (yesterday): the Metropolitan Development Commission gave final approval,
  unanimously (6-0 of members present)**, adopting it as zoning ordinance **2026-AO-001**. This
  was the last procedural step — council passage alone wasn't self-executing; it needed MDC
  sign-off to take effect. Mayor Joe Hogsett (D) supported it.
  [Fox59](https://fox59.com/news/metropolitan-development-commission-unanimously-approves-data-center-moratorium/), [WTHR](https://www.wthr.com/article/news/local/indianapolis-data-center-moratorium-takes-effect-passage-metropolitan-development-commission/531-1517b382-2202-40bb-8fa5-6b1947a6bf4d), [WFYI](https://www.wfyi.org/wfyi-news/2026-08-19/new-data-centers-temporarily-banned-in-marion-county), [WIBC](https://wibc.com/894987/data-center-moratorium-unanimously-passes-in-marion-county/)
- **Effect: no new data center permitting in Marion County through Dec. 31, 2027.**
- **Important carve-out: the moratorium is not retroactive.** Three already-approved projects
  are explicitly exempt: **DC Blox** (Warren Township/Irvington), **Metrobloks**
  (Martindale-Brightwood), and **Sabey Corp.** (southwestern Marion County/Decatur Township) —
  see #2 below, this is a project not in the corpus or in either the 08-16 or 08-19 notes at all.

**Read for the paper:** this is a clean, dated confirmation of the pattern 08-19 flagged as
possibly significant — a city-level legislative body (council) succeeding with a moratorium
*after* losing at the development-commission level on individual site approvals (DC Blox,
Metrobloks), consistent with the "different venue succeeds where another didn't" dynamic already
noted for NY's statewide executive order. The exemption of already-approved projects is itself a
data point worth keeping: the moratorium regulates *future* permitting, not the sites that drew
the opposition in the first place, which is a real limit on what these legislative wins actually
deliver to the residents who organized around them — a nuance worth keeping in mind if this
becomes a "opposition win" exemplar, since it's a partial win at best for the specific
communities involved (Irvington, Martindale-Brightwood, Decatur Township residents don't get
their site-level fights reopened).

## 2. New finding: a third, previously-undocumented Indianapolis data-center opposition case — Sabey/Decatur Township, with active litigation

Neither the 08-16 scan nor the 08-19 sourcing pass mentioned this. It surfaced only because the
Aug 19 MDC vote coverage named all three exempted projects.

- **Sabey Data Centers** proposes a **~130-acre, ~$4 billion** hyperscale campus near Kentucky
  Ave/Camby Road in **Decatur Township**, southwest Indianapolis — described as Sabey's largest
  data hub yet and the city's first hyperscale data center.
- **Approval mechanism is itself contested**: Sabey sought a *variance of use* rather than a
  full rezoning. That distinction matters procedurally — a rezoning would let the City-County
  Council "call down" the MDC's decision and vote on it directly regardless of what MDC decided;
  a variance route avoids that council check. Residents and coverage frame this as a way the
  project sidestepped the more public, council-level process that DC Blox and Metrobloks both
  went through.
  [WTHR](https://www.wthr.com/article/news/local/proposed-decatur-township-data-center-sparks-opposition-bypasses-city-county-council-vote/531-316120a8-0588-4dd2-96de-9bfa0baa5dcd)
- **MDC approved the zoning adjustment March 18, 2026.**
- **Litigation followed**: seven Decatur Township residents filed a petition April 17, 2026 for
  judicial review of the MDC's March 18 decision — a grassroots group called **Protect Decatur
  Township** organized around rising electric-bill concerns, infrastructure strain, property
  values, and pollution. [WFYI](https://www.wfyi.org/wfyi-news/2026-04-20/decatur-township-residents-sue-to-block-sabey-data-center), [Mirror Indy](https://mirrorindy.org/decatur-township-civic-council-sabey-data-center-judicial-review-metropolitan-development-commission/), [WTHR](https://www.wthr.com/article/news/local/indianapolis-residents-take-sabey-data-center-fight-to-court-after-city-approval-decatur-township/531-7178f971-4a00-43a7-9300-19203a76d5a0)
- Outcome of the judicial-review petition not established by this search pass — worth a

  question this note was chasing.
- Not independently verified beyond these search-summary hits; flagging as a lead, same
