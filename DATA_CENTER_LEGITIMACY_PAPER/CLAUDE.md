# "The Cloud Has a Zip Code" — Project Brief

## What this is
A conceptual/theory paper, separate from `DATA_CENTER_PAPER` (the multi-state
public-opposition thematic-analysis project). Both share the data-center topic
and can likely share corpus/real-world grounding material, but this is a
distinct manuscript with its own model, propositions, and target journal —
adopted as its own thread 2026-09-10.

**Working title:** "The Cloud Has a Zip Code: Benefit–Burden Asymmetry and the
Social Legitimacy of AI Infrastructure"

**Target journal:** Corporate Social Responsibility and Environmental
Management (CSREM, Wiley)

## The conceptual model
- Diffuse societal benefits + concentrated local burdens → **benefit–burden
  asymmetry (BBA)**
- BBA (−) → distributive justice (P1)
- BBA (−) → procedural justice (P2)
- Distributive justice (+) → infrastructure legitimacy / social license (P3)
- Procedural justice (+) → infrastructure legitimacy / social license (P4)
- **Benefit localization** (moderator): weakens the BBA → distributive justice
  relationship (P5)
- **Institutional distance from burden** (moderator): strengthens the BBA →
  procedural justice relationship (P6) — construct label under active
  scrutiny, see literature audit
- Distinguishes **objective BBA** (structural/measurable) from **perceived
  BBA** (evaluative)
- BBA has four candidate diagnostic dimensions: geographic dispersion, scale
  mismatch, visibility asymmetry, temporal mismatch — status of each under
  active review, see literature audit
