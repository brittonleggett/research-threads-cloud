1. ✅ **RESOLVED 2026-09-04 via Consensus.app.** The "+22.8%"-style claim traces to a real, peer-reviewed paper: Ozer, Greenwood & Gopal, "Let it Ride! An Empirical Investigation of Problem Gambling and the Implications of Legalized Online Sports Betting," *Information Systems Research* (2026), DOI 10.1287/isre.2024.1526. Confirmed: online legalization (not physical/retail) is strongly correlated with increased problem-gambling helpline calls and with increased state suicides, concentrated among young, unmarried, less-educated men. See `literature/literature_map.md` Category C — now a fully-verified core anchor citation, not a claim to verify.

This peer-reviewed-vs-preprint contradiction is the real, citable version of "present conflicting evidence" for this outcome — see literature map.
